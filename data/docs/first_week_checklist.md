# First Week Checklist for New Engineers

## Day 1
Collect your laptop from the IT desk on Floor 2 between 9:30 AM and 11:00 AM. You will receive access to GitHub, Slack, Jira, PagerDuty and the GlobalConnect VPN. Meet your onboarding buddy, who is assigned before you join.

## Days 2 and 3
Follow the Local Setup Guide until `make test` passes on your machine. Then browse Jira for tickets with the label `good-first-issue`.

## End of week 1
Open your first pull request by Friday. Attend the weekly architecture walkthrough with the Platform Team every Thursday at 4:00 PM.

## Within 14 days
Complete the mandatory security awareness training. Start shadowing the on-call rotation.

## Useful Slack channels
`#eng-announcements` for news, `#deploys` for release activity, `#ask-platform` for platform questions and `#it-help` for laptop or access problems.
